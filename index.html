About Neon

Neon turns English into American Sign Language. Type a sentence or speak it, and the figure on the left signs it.

  • Known words (like HELLO, NAME, THANK-YOU) are signed as whole signs.
  • Everything else, such as names, is fingerspelled letter by letter.
  • With a Gemini key, AI rewrites your sentence into ASL word order first. Without one, simple built-in rules do it.
  • Question mode (needs a key): ask a question, Gemini writes a short answer, and Neon signs it.

Signs are hand-authored and approximate. ASL also uses the face and body, which Neon does not show yet. Treat it as a learning aid, not a replacement for a human interpreter.

Privacy: your key stays in this browser and is only sent to Google's Gemini API. Speech input in Chrome is processed by Google's speech service.
